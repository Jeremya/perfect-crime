# [X11] Share a failure — "caught on heist #3, do better"

- **Epic**: X — experience · **Role**: Dev · **Estimate**: M
- **Depends on**: C4, E2, E4 — merged · **Branch**: `x11-share-failure` · **PR**: `[X11] Share a failure`
- **Status**: Proposal (cut from the review of 2026-11-09). The spec has a single Share button, after a win: this is an addition, so it needs the product owner's go.

## Why

Only winners can share. A player who fails six times and leaves has no button, and they are the ones with a story: "Spotted by the camera" under a red stamp is the funniest frame of the game. A failure gives nothing away, since it shows a plan that does not work.

## Scope

- From the **third failure** on the same heist (the moment the first hint appears), the failure screen offers a secondary "Dare a friend" action next to "tap to return". Never before: the first failures must lead back to the plan, not out of the game.
- What gets sent:

  ```
  Daily Heist #3 🕵️ caught 3 times
  👁️👁️📷 · still at large?
  Do better: https://<domain>/c/3
  ```

  The link carries **no payload**: there is no result to compare with. It opens the heist of that day, without a challenge card.
- Daily heists only. No share on a tutorial failure.
- `share_tapped` with a new `context: 'failure'`; `share_result` unchanged. `docs/dashboard.md` counts it apart from the spontaneous-sharing indicator, which stays defined on wins, so the Gate 1 threshold keeps its meaning.

## Not in X11

- An image of the fatal frame. It would show part of a route and it depends on the `image` variant surviving E5.

## Acceptance criteria

- No action on failures 1 and 2; the action on failure 3 and after.
- A tap anywhere else still returns to the intact plan, as today.
- The link has no fragment and opens the same heist on another device.
- The action does not cover the fatal cell, the stamp or the cause (UI-DESIGN: nothing obscures the board).
