# [X13] The express tutorial: measure the loss, then shorten it

- **Epic**: X — experience · **Role**: PM + Dev · **Estimate**: S (measure) + S (variant)
- **Depends on**: E4, G1 — merged · **Branch**: `x13-express-funnel` · **PR**: `[X13] The express tutorial funnel`
- **Status**: Proposal (cut from the review of 2026-11-09). Step 2 changes the spec's arrival flow and needs the product owner's go; step 1 does not.

## Why

A friend who taps a challenge link was promised one heist. A new player first gets the challenge card, then T1, T2 and T4 (about 90 seconds by the spec) before the heist they came for. Every level is a place to leave, on the path that decides K at gate 2. Nobody knows today how many leave there.

## Step 1 — measure (no behaviour change)

- A funnel in `docs/dashboard.md` from the existing events: `link_opened` → `challenge_cta` (play) → `level_start`/`attempt_win` on t1, t2, t4 → `level_start` on the linked heist → `challenge_result`.
- If the events cannot tell an express run from the normal tutorial, add `express: boolean` to `level_start` (the route already knows: `expressDay`).

## Step 2 — a shorter variant, behind a flag

- `?flag=express:1` (default: today's three levels) sends a new player through **T2 only**, then the linked heist. T1's lesson (draw to the diamond, then the exit) is carried by the first-launch hand on T2; T4's lesson (tap to pause) by the hint that the linked heist already offers after three failures.
- The flag is remembered in the profile and sent with the events, as the spec asks for flags, so the two groups can be compared.

## Decision rule

Keep the three levels unless the short variant brings clearly more players to `challenge_result` without lowering the win rate of the linked heist. With 20–30 warm testers this will only show a large gap; the real comparison is for gate 2.

## Ownership — create/edit only these files

- Step 1: `docs/dashboard.md`, `src/app/analytics.ts`, `src/game/level-scene.ts` (the `level_start` call), `tests/unit/analytics.test.ts`
- Step 2: `src/app/router.ts`, `src/app/config.ts`, `tests/unit/router.test.ts`, `tests/unit/link-arrival.test.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- The dashboard shows where link arrivals stop, level by level.
- With the flag on, a new player reaches the linked heist after one level; a returning player is unaffected.

## Definition of done

Full local check green; the funnel shows test data on the live dashboard.
