# [E6] Comparison after the linked heist, and the rematch

- **Epic**: E — Sharing and challenges · **Role**: Dev · **Estimate**: S
- **Depends on**: E2, E4 — merged · **Branch**: `e6-comparison` · **PR**: `[E6] Comparison and rematch`
- **Status**: In review. Left: the preview on two phones. `challenge_result` was declared but never sent; the flow now sends it on the first win of a taken challenge.

## Why

The spec's arrival flow ends on the comparison: "4. Comparison: attempts first, then ticks", then "5. 'Share my result' sends a link back in turn: that is the rematch." The screen's actions are See their plan, Share my result, Home.

In the game, "Take the challenge" opens the heist without remembering the challenge (`yapChallengeHeistRoute`), and the win leads to Home (`yapRouteAfterWin`). The comparison only shows if the player opens the same link again, and it still offers Take the challenge and Later. `share_tapped` with `context: 'comparison'` can never fire. Sharing from the Win screen works, so the rematch exists, but the moment built to trigger it does not. Whether people share is one of the three questions of Gate 1.

## Task

- Keep the challenge on the route, so that leaving the Win screen of a linked heist opens the comparison.
- With a result for that day, the actions become See their plan, Share my result (`comparison.shareMyResult`), Home (`comparison.home`). Without one, they stay Take the challenge and Later.
- Share my result sends the player's own link, with `context: 'comparison'`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Link opened, heist won, Win screen left: the comparison shows, with the verdict.
- Share my result sends a link whose payload is the recipient's own result.
- The friend's plan rule does not change.
