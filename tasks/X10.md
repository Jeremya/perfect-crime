# [X10] The nerve line — a share result that tells the heist

- **Epic**: X — experience · **Role**: Dev · **Estimate**: M
- **Depends on**: C5, E2 — merged · **Branch**: `x10-nerve-line` · **PR**: `[X10] The nerve line in the share`
- **Status**: Proposal (cut from the review of 2026-11-09). It changes the spec's "What gets sent", so it needs the product owner's go before any code.

## Why

"Do people share it?" is one of the three questions of Gate 1, and the shared text is the only thing a friend sees. Today it is `👁️👁️📷✅ · 41 ticks ⭐⭐`: one emoji per attempt. It is short, it looks like nothing in a chat, and two players with the same attempt count send the same line. Wordle's grid spreads because it tells the game at a glance and no two are alike.

The solver already counts **tension**: the ticks of a plan where the thief stands one step from a seen cell (`tensionOf` in `src/core/solver.ts`). The same measure, per tick of the player's winning plan, gives a line that is theirs:

```
Daily Heist #3 🕵️ cracked in 4 tries
⬜⬜🟧⬜🟧🟧⬜💎⬜⬜🟧⬜🚪
22 moves ⭐⭐⭐
Your turn: https://<domain>/c/3#AQME…
```

One square per tick: ⬜ calm, 🟧 one step from a seen cell, 💎 the tick of the diamond, 🚪 the exit.

## Open questions, to settle before coding

1. **Does it spoil?** The line gives the plan's length, the tick of the diamond and when the thief was close to danger. It gives no cell and no direction. Check on d3 and d5 (the signature heists) that a player cannot rebuild the route from it; if they can, drop 💎 or bucket the ticks in pairs.
2. **Length.** Plans go up to 64 ticks; above 24 squares the line wraps in WhatsApp. Proposal: one square per 2 ticks above 24, per 3 above 48.
3. **The attempts line.** Keep `👁️👁️📷✅` on the title line, or let the count in the title carry it.
4. **"Ticks".** The word is in the share text and on every screen. "Moves" / "coups" needs no explanation. A wording change in both dictionaries; the spec says ticks.

## Scope

- A pure function in `src/game/win.ts`, from the level and the winning plan, through `resolveTick` and `visibleCells` only: no rule computed outside `src/core/`.
- The Win screen's telegram shows the line that will be sent.
- The Home share (`context: 'home'`) rebuilds it from the stored plan of the day record.
- Behind `?share=` as a fourth variant (`nerve`), so E5 can compare it with `text` on real phones before it becomes the default.

## Acceptance criteria

- Two different winning plans of the same heist give two different lines; the same plan always gives the same line.
- The line of every optimal plan of d1–d7 fits on one row at 360 px in WhatsApp (to record in `docs/share-matrix.md`).
- No cell coordinate can be read from the text; the spoiler check of question 1 is written down.
- `share_result` reports the variant.

## Definition of done

Full local check green, `npm run test:e2e` green, one real send recorded per target app.
