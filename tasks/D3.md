## Why

The spec lists a Settings screen ("Language, sound, vibration, optional nickname, editable consent, privacy page, version"), reached from Home, and one Phaser scene for it. No story owned it: A3, C8, E1 and G2 each built their part, and the screen itself fell between them. The game ships without it:

- a player who answered the consent banner can never change the answer, and "Learn more" opens two lines in the banner, not a privacy page;
- sound and vibration cannot be turned off;
- the language is the browser's, with no way to change it;
- the nickname is always empty, so every challenge card says "A friend".

The first two must be fixed before anyone outside the team tests the game (H5).

## Already in the code

`yapI18n.setLocale` and `yapLanguageLabel` (no reload), `yapSoundSettings`, `yapSetConsent`, the `settings.*` keys and `home.settings` in both dictionaries, the payload's nickname field (12 bytes at most, `YAP_MAX_NICKNAME_BYTES`).

## Task

- A settings entry on Home that leaves the three bottom actions alone.
- Rows, in the spec's order, in the plan style: language, sound, vibration, nickname, consent, privacy, version. Every change applies at once and is remembered.
- Consent: changing it to refused stops every event from then on; a `consent_set` follows the same rules as the banner's. Refusing must take no more taps than accepting.
- Privacy: a page reachable from Settings and from "Learn more": what is measured, what is not, the PostHog EU host, deletion at the end of Gate 2, who to contact.
- Nickname: in shared links only, never in analytics (spec, "Analytics and privacy").

## Still needs a decision

The page names Yap Corp. It has no contact until `YAP_CORP.contact` is set: the section appears by itself. An IP address reaches PostHog with every request, and the banner says "no personal data": "Discard client IP data" must be on in the PostHog project, which nobody has checked yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every item of the spec's Settings row works on a 360 × 640 phone, in French and in English.
- A refusal given in Settings means zero events after it, checked on the network.
- A nickname set here shows on the challenge card of a link shared afterwards.

## Definition of done

Full local check green, `npm run test:e2e` green, preview checked on a real phone.
