## Why

The board at rest is tick 0. On d7, 17 cells are dangerous at tick 0 and 35 over its 24-tick cycle; on d3, 11 and 25. Half of the danger is not on screen when the player starts drawing, and the tick order (the thief moves, the guard moves and turns, then detection) is invisible. Players read it as "the guard saw me for no reason". The Gate 1 spec asks that a solution can be deduced from what is visible and from the ghost preview, never from random trial and error.

The throwaway branch `spike-observer` tested the answer on the Seeker: one time control, shared before and after the route.

## Scope

1. **Watch the round, before the route.** 👁 Observer plays one cycle of the world (guards, cameras, lasers) at 2 ticks/s, the thief on the start, with a tick counter, Pause/Play, −1, +1 and Close. A line says how often everything repeats. No button when the world never changes (cycle 1).
2. **Walk the plan, after the route.** The same control runs the thief on the drawn plan and stops at the first detection, on the fatal cell, with the cause in one sentence. −1 shows the tick before, so the player sees what a pause would change.
3. **The tick band, while drawing.** While the finger is down, a band replaces the header: 12 ticks around the arrival on the cell under the finger, crossed where the cell is exposed, and the threat in words.
4. **X3 stays.** The quick flight keeps its own button and its behaviour: Observer walks, Preview flies. The spike merged the two modes; here Observer is a separate mode, so the X3 code path does not change.
5. **Night stays off.** `?flag=night:1` remains a dev flag, off by default. It breaks the neutral plan of `docs/UI-DESIGN.md` and suggests that dark cells are safe.

The bottom row of planning:

| State | Row |
|---|---|
| Unfinished route | Clear · Observer · disabled PLAY |
| Unfinished route with a hint | Clear · Hint · Observer (in the slot of the disabled PLAY) |
| Finished route | Clear 72 · Observer 80 · Preview 72 · PLAY |
| Cycle 1 | As today, no Observer |

Also in scope:

- `?flag=observer:0/1`, default on (decided), same syntax as `?flag=preview:`, so H3 can compare with and without.
- One `observer_open` event through `track()`, with `level_id`, `kind` (`round` or `plan`) and `attempt`, so the test week tells whether players use it.
- Under reduced motion, Observer opens paused on tick 0.

## Not in X9

- A mark on the next step of each guard.
- The tick order said in T4 ("you move, then the guard moves, then it looks").
- A "danger at tick 0 against the cycle" metric in `validate`.

Each one is its own decision, after H1.

## To fix while porting

- **Kit:** `fireOn: 'up'` fires on any release over a zone, even if the press began elsewhere. The spike only moved Observer and Hint to `up`. Fix it in `src/game/kit.ts`: a button fires only if it took the press.
- **Revisited cells (new since #69):** a route can pass twice on a cell. The band and the plan walk must use the most recent pass, like a tap does.
- **Contact:** a swap with a guard stays invisible to the band, which says "no sight or beam" and never promises a safe cell. Keep that wording.
- **Header:** it says "in progress" during an observation.
- **Rebuild:** the band disappears if the scene rebuilds during a stroke.
- **360 px French:** the two-threat label has not been seen on screen.
