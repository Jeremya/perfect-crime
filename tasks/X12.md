## Why

A challenge card reads "Alex cleared heist #3 in 2 attempts. Your turn." only if Alex opened Settings and typed a name. Nobody will, so every card says "A friend cleared…". A named challenge is a message from someone; an anonymous one is an ad.

## Scope

- On the Win screen, while the nickname is empty, a one-line field sits above Share: "Sign your heist (optional)". It reuses the Settings field (`yapOpenField`) and `yapSaveNickname`, so the 12-byte rule and the cleaning stay in one place.
- Share works with the field left empty, in one tap, exactly as today. The field never blocks, never opens the keyboard by itself, and never shows again once a nickname is set or after the player has shared once without one.
- The nickname still only leaves the device inside shared links. Nothing new in analytics, except one boolean `has_nickname` on `share_tapped`, to know whether named challenges convert better (`challenge_cta`).

## Constraint

`navigator.share` needs the tap's own activation: nothing may be awaited between the tap on Share and the call. The field is therefore filled **before** the tap, never in a dialog after it.

## Acceptance criteria

- Empty nickname: the field shows; a typed name is in the payload of the link sent by the next tap on Share.
- Share with the field empty still sends in one tap.
- The Win report still fits at 360 × 640 with the field, in French.
- The privacy page stays true: the nickname is in no event.
